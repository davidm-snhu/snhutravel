# How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

To turn user needs into a piece of concrete actionable work to do, you need to translate from one frame of mind to another without losing track of the original intent. User stories provide concrete acceptance criteria and restate the original user need. They also include a field for roughly how large the work is estimated to be, which helps prioritize what is implemented first and what's saved for later or not done.

# What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

I think that being a good team member might mean keeping friction low for people whose tasks have to mesh with your own: documenting your work so that people using or extending it later don't need to rediscover what it does and why, updating people whos work may be waiting on yours so that they can prioritize and organize their own tasks, maintaining good rapport with coworkers so that the experience of working together continues to feel possitive.
